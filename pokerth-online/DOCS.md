# PokerTH Online

This add-on adds a **PokerTH Online** entry to the Home Assistant sidebar that embeds the official hosted web client, https://webclient.pokerth.net — the same client the PokerTH project runs on pokerth.net.

Nothing is hosted on your box: your browser talks directly to the official servers. That means it works both on your LAN and remotely (Nabu Casa or any HA remote access), and it needs no ports, no options and virtually no resources.

Use the "Open in tab ↗" button (top right) to play in a full browser tab — recommended on phones, and the fallback if the embedded view ever refuses to load.

## Which add-on do I want?

- **PokerTH Online** (this one) — zero-install shortcut to the official hosted client. Works remotely.
- **PokerTH Web Client** — self-hosted client + proxy on your box, with its own admin page and LAN server support. LAN only.
- **PokerTH Server** — a dedicated game server on your box.
